Our Initiatives &
Moonlight Seva

Like moonlight that illuminates without demanding attention, every initiative of the Koumudi Foundation is designed to quietly restore dignity, opportunity, and hope to those who need it most. Selfless service — Seva — is our highest calling.

Initiative I · Vidya Seva

Girl Child & Underprivileged Education

Education is the single most reliable pathway out of poverty, yet millions of children — especially girls — are forced to abandon school due to financial hardship. The Koumudi Foundation intervenes directly and discreetly.

check_circle School fees & tuition paid directly to institutions
check_circle Books, uniforms, stationery kits provided in-kind
check_circle Mentorship and academic follow-through support
check_circle Priority focus on grade 8 dropout prevention

Initiative II · Swasthya Seva

Kidney Health & Renal Patient Support

Chronic Kidney Disease silently devastates millions of families. With recurring dialysis costs exceeding ₹12,000–₹18,000 per month, low-income patients face an impossible choice between treatment and survival.

check_circle Dialysis session subsidies paid to partner hospitals
check_circle Essential nephrology medications support
check_circle AV Fistula and vascular access surgery assistance
check_circle Free community renal screening camps quarterly
