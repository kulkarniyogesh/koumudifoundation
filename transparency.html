Assistance Eligibility Policy

All aid applications are assessed solely on objective need. No caste, religion, gender, or political consideration influences the review. See the full 3-phase verification process on our Save Kidney page and Aims & Objectives.

Phase 1

Clinical documentation from treating nephrologist

Phase 2

Socio-economic field verification by foundation social workers

Phase 3

Unanimous trustee approval and direct hospital remittance
